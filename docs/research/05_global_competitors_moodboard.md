## 🎨 Global Design & Management Patterns Summary

Across these 40 premier institutions, the dominant digital patterns are:

1. **Brand Storytelling & Video/Hero Imagery:**
   - Instead of static generic classrooms, homepages feature high-production cinematic campus video backdrops or bold editorial photo grids.
   - Slogans focus on transformation: *"Every child known", "Future leaders", "Curiosity into Impact"*.

2. **Management & Admissions Software Dominance:**
   - **OpenApply / Faria:** Dominates international admissions tracking in Asia, Europe, and Middle East.
   - **Veracross & Blackbaud:** Industry standard across top US and UK preparatory academies for student records, parent portals, and tuition billing.
   - **iSAMS & Engage:** Popular across British international schools for academic routine management, gradebooks, and parent apps.
   - **Toddle & ManageBac:** Leading learning management systems for progressive/IB curricula.

3. **Color & Typography Trends:**
   - **Deep Regal Palettes:** Navy Blue (`#0F2537`), Royal Crimson (`#800020`), Forest Green (`#1B4332`), paired with warm cream or crisp white backgrounds.
   - **Typography Pairings:** Distinctive Serif headings (Playfair, Garamond, Cinzel) with clean, modern Sans-Serif body copy (Inter, Figtree, Plus Jakarta Sans) to project both academic heritage and modern agility.
