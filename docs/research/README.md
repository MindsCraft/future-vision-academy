## 🎯 Quick Executive Summary

1. **The Core Audience:** Tech-savvy urban parents in Bangladesh seeking academic excellence, safety, and modern infrastructure. Over **80% of browsing traffic happens on smartphones** over mobile data.
2. **The "Must-Have" Pillars:**
   - **Real-Time Notice Board:** The most heavily visited section on Bangladeshi school websites. Must support PDF viewing without requiring excessive downloading.
   - **Frictionless Online Admission:** Step-by-step form with document upload (birth certificate, parent NID, passport photos) and instant application tracking.
   - **Local MFS Payment Gateways:** Direct payment options via bKash and Nagad for application fees and tuition payments.
   - **Institutional Transparency:** Clear display of EIIN, board recognition, campus facilities, leadership credentials, and fee structures.
3. **Competitive Opportunity for Future Vision Academy:**
   - Most established school websites in Bangladesh suffer from dated UI (mid-2000s layouts), sluggish page loads, lack of mobile optimization, and fragmented third-party portals.
   - By leveraging a fast, modern Next.js 16 / React 19 stack with clean responsive styling, Future Vision Academy can present a world-class, premium digital experience that immediately stands out and commands parental trust.
